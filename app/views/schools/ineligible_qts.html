You are not eligible for this payment

You are not eligible for a targeted retention incentive payment because you were awarded qualified teacher status (QTS) or qualified teacher learning and skills (QTLS) status on or after 1 September 2026.

To claim this year, you must have been awarded QTS or QTLS before 1 September 2026.

For more information, check the eligibility criteria for school targeted retention incentives.

If you think this is wrong, email schools-targeted.retention-incentive@education.gov.uk.
