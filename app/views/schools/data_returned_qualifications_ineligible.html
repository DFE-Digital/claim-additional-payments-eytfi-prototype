You are not eligible for this payment

You are not eligible for a targeted retention incentive payment because you do not have an eligible qualification.

To be eligible, you must have one of these:

  • an initial teacher training (ITT) course specialising in chemistry, computing, mathematics or physics
  • an undergraduate or postgraduate degree in chemistry, computing, mathematics or physics

For more information, check the eligibility criteria for school targeted retention incentives.
