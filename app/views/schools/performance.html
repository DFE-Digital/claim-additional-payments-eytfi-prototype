Check if you’re eligible

You must meet the eligibility criteria (opens in new tab) for the targeted retention incentive payment for school teachers.

To be eligible, you must:

  • be employed directly by your school, not through an agency, and have worked there for at least one term if you’re a supply teacher
  • be doing your job as expected and not be under formal performance management or disciplinary action
