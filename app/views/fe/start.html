Targeted retention incentive payment for further education teachers

Use this service to claim a targeted retention incentive payment. The payment is only for certain further education (FE) teachers in the first 5 years of their teaching career.

What you need

You'll need:

  • a GOV.UK One Login account to prove your identity - you'll need a UK passport, UK photo driving licence or biometric residence permit to set it up
  • your bank details, including the full name on the account, sort code and account number
  • your teaching timetable
  • a contract or written statement of employment

Before you start

If you need to set up a GOV.UK One Login account, this can take 2 weeks.

If you applied last year

We review eligibility every year. You will need to make a new claim each time you apply. DfE retains data from previous claims, which may be referenced during your application.

How long it takes to claim

If you have your One Login account it takes around X minutes to submit a claim.

What happens after you submit a claim

You'll get an email to tell you if you're eligible.

If you're eligible, you'll be paid within 19 weeks.

Contact us

If you have any questions, email FE-targeted.retention-incentive@education.gov.uk .
