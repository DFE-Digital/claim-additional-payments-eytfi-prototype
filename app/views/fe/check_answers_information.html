How we'll use your information

To process your application

We will check the information you have provided with the:

  • further education provider where you currently teach
  • the Student Loans Company

Student loan deductions

We'll check if you're repaying a student loan. If you are, we'll take the right amount from your recognition payment automatically.

Income Tax

We'll check if you pay the higher rate of Income Tax. If you do, we'll take any extra tax you owe from your payment. You do not need to do anything.

To pay you

The Department of Education (DfE) will make the payment directly to you. To complete the rest of your application, you'll need your:

  • National Insurance number
  • bank account details
