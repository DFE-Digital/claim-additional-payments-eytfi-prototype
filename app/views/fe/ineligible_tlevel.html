You are not eligible for a targeted retention incentive payment yet

To claim a targeted retention incentive payment, at least half of your timetabled teaching hours must be spent teaching students on 16 to 19 study programmes, T Levels or apprentices aged 16 to 19.

This includes teaching:

  • students enrolled on these programmes who are up to 25 years old with an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP)
  • 14 to 16 year olds who are enrolled on 16 to 19 funded study programmes

For more information, check the eligibility criteria for targeted retention incentive payments for further education teachers (opens in new tab).

If you are unsure your information is correct, you can go back and review your answers. You can also start the application again.
